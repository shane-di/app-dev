#FAVORITE SERIES: **Twenty-Five Twenty-One**

*Twenty-Five Twenty-One* or *스물다섯 스물하나* is a South Korean Drama that is aired from February 12, 2022 to April 3, 2022 with 16 episodes.

The main cast in the story are:

| Actor/Actress | Character |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Kim Tae-ri | Na Hee-do |
| Nam Joo-hyuk | Baek Yi-jin |
| Bona | Ko Yu-rim |

The story begins when Na Hee-do's daughter, Kim Min-chae, saw her mothers diary during her school days. In the diary, it showcases all of Na Hee-do's journey, rants, heartbreaks, and as well as her dreams to become a professional fencer. 
She was idolizing Ko Yu-rim from afar and wanted to become like her, who is already a great fencer that is a representative of the other school. But at that time, there was this crisis called **IMF financial crisis** where Na Hee-do's school with fencing club is about to disband due to financial difficulties. With the said crisis, she decided to transfer from other school with fencing club which is Ko Yu-rim's school.

Her first fight with Yu-rim isn't good since Yu-rim looks down on Hee-do and there was a tension between them. Yu-rim thinks that Hee Do is a threat to her dream since she shows her great skills in playing.

Hee-do is trying her best to prove herself that she can be the best fencer and here is where she met Baek Yi-jin. They began to bond together and Yi-jin always supports her to her aspirations despite having his own struggles.  

As Hee-do continuously train herself, she overcomes obstacles and secures a slot on the national fencing team. Meanwhile, Yu-rim  feels pressure and starts to recognize Hee-do as her greatest rival and has the potential to surpass her as the best fencer. Until, there is a moment where they have to be in the same documentary about fencing. At first, they feel uncomfortable but, as time goes by, they understand each other and from being competitors, they became friends. 

From trying their best to become represenatives of their school, they become into an elite fencer that represents their country on international stage. Hee-do representing South Korea feel heartbroken and feels abandoned since  Yu-rim or *Julia Ko* represents Russia since she saw that Russia can offer her financial support and sponsorships. They became international rivals on the Asian Games Match. The match was intense but they were both acknowldeged that whatever their teams are, they are forever bonded.
